# Clinical sponsor / doctor commissioning worksheet (complete before any patient-data pilot)

This is for a clinician and hospital governance team to fill out. The VIGIL software is **not** approved for patient care.

1. **Clinical sponsor and accountable institution:** name, department, hospital, contact through approved channels.
2. **Precisely intended task:** e.g., archived ECG review / clinician-assisted beat localization. **NOT** “all biomedical analysis”. What exact output must be supported?
3. **Target patients and use setting:** age groups, inpatient/outpatient/ICU, procedures, inclusion/exclusion, hardware sources.
4. **Intended user and qualifications:** cardiologist, clinical physiologist, biomedical engineer, trained nurse; what training is required?
5. **Clinical decision and hazard:** Will the software inform, drive or make a decision? What could happen if it misses a beat, flags noise, or misidentifies a patient?
6. **Reference standard:** qualified adjudicators, disagreement resolution, data rights, prospective versus retrospective collection.
7. **Pre-agreed acceptance criteria:** define each metric and minimum thresholds with clinical justification **before** evaluating an independent test set.
8. **Data governance:** approved dataset paths, de-identification standard, consent or lawful basis, cross-border transfers, retention, breach response, ethics determination.
9. **Operating environment:** offline workstation or hospital-network integration, approved OS, accounts, backups, encryption, time sync, restore/recovery and incident escalation.
10. **Regulatory/QMS owners:** manufacturer-of-record, quality manager, security contact, regulatory assessment, local authority and clinical investigation pathway.
11. **Study authorization:** approved protocol ID, ethics determination, where needed permission for clinical investigation, monitoring and adverse-event reporting.
12. **Approval:** version/date of intended-use statement, signatures from clinical, software, quality, security and regulatory leads.

Do not attach or upload identifiable patient records here or to third-party services. Use hospital-approved storage and processes.
